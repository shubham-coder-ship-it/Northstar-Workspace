01 · FEATURES

Choose how you trade.

Practice Mode

Start with virtual USDT. Enter a reference price, simulate market and limit orders, and track your holdings and realized P/L.

Live Mode

Connect Binance USDⓈ-M Futures to see available USDT and place market or limit orders after reviewing the order details.

Account & Billing

Manage your profile and optional Northstar membership. Stripe hosts subscription checkout and billing.

02 · NORTHSTAR ADVANTAGE

Practice and live trading stay separate.

Northstar keeps virtual balances and order history separate from live orders. Live credentials are encrypted, and sending a real-money order requires explicit confirmation.

04 · HELP

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Binance API key?

Practice Mode works without a key. Live Mode requires a production Binance HMAC key with USDⓈ-M Futures access.

How does Practice Mode work?

Practice Mode uses virtual USDT. Enter a reference price to simulate orders and track your virtual balance, holdings, and realized profit or loss.

When does Northstar place a real order?

In Live Mode, review your market or limit order and type LIVE to authorize it. Switching modes does not place an order.

Do I need a subscription to trade?

No. The optional Northstar membership adds a downloadable CSV of your Practice and Live order history.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open Account & Billing and select Manage subscription to open the Stripe billing portal.

How do I reset my password?

Select Forgot Password on the sign-in screen. Northstar can send a reset link when the workspace operator has configured email delivery.
